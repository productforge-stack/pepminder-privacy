Where Regimen wins

  • Complex schedules like every 3.5 days, and reminders before each dose change.
  • Analysis: estimated level curves, "Signals" trend detection, and bloodwork overlays.
  • Health Connect and Apple Health integration.
  • Cross-device sync and a web app.

Where PepMinder wins

  • Offline and private. No account, and your data is never on a server. Regimen reviewers specifically complain that nothing loads without internet.
  • More free, for less. 2 peptides free (versus 1), Pro is cheaper at $39.99/yr versus $49.99/yr, and you can pay $49.99 once instead of every year.
  • Titration without paying. Dose steps are free in PepMinder; the reminder on a change day says which step starts.
  • Simpler. Fewer screens between you and a logged dose.

Which should you pick?

  • Choose Regimen if you want deeper level analysis, every-3.5-day schedules, or lab analysis, and you're comfortable with an account and a connection.
  • Choose PepMinder if you want reliable reminders (titration included), accurate syringe math, and a clean log that's private and works anywhere.
