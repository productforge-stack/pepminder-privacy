## Where Smart Peptide Tracker wins

- **Breadth.** Half-life graphs, cost tracking, biomarkers, partner profiles, and an AI assistant. If you want one app for everything, it's hard to beat.
- **A cheaper one-time price.** Its Premium is listed at $34.99 (often discounted) versus PepMinder's $49.99 lifetime, though some features are paid add-ons.
- **iPhone and web**, today.

## Where PepMinder wins

- **Both can be paid once.** PepMinder's Lifetime Pro ($49.99) unlocks everything, with no add-ons.
- **Simplicity.** The most common complaint in Smart Peptide Tracker reviews is that it's busy and hard to learn. PepMinder is built to get you from opening the app to a logged dose in seconds.
- **Privacy.** No account, no sign-up, no cloud. Your dosing history never leaves your phone.
- **Works fully offline**, including reminders, the calculator, and logs.
- **"Suggest for easy measurement"**, which picks a water amount so your dose lands on a clean syringe mark.

## Which should you pick?

- Choose **Smart Peptide Tracker** if you want every feature, cost tracking, or AI help, and you're fine with an account.
- Choose **PepMinder** if you want a clean, private tracker that does the essentials well, on Android, without signing up.

You can try PepMinder's free tier alongside any app you already use. There's nothing to sign up for.
