# A peptide tracker with no account

Most peptide apps want your email before they'll show you a calculator. Then your dosing history, which is some of the most personal data you have, lives on their server.

**PepMinder works differently. There is no account. There's nothing to sign up for, and your data stays on your phone.**

## Why it matters

- **It's your medication history.** A list of what you inject, how much, and when isn't something you should have to hand to a company to get a reminder.
- **No server means nothing to breach.** Data that's never uploaded can't leak from someone else's database.
- **No connection needed.** Reminders, the calculator, and your log work on a plane, in a gym basement, or anywhere else with no signal.
- **No lock-in.** Export to CSV or PDF, or make a full backup file, whenever you want.

## What stays on your phone

| Data | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| Peptides, vials, and concentrations | Your phone |
| Schedules and reminders | Your phone (reminders are scheduled on the device) |
| Dose log and injection sites | Your phone |
| Weight entries and progress photos | Your phone |
| Backups | Wherever *you* save the file |

## The honest fine print

PepMinder's free version shows **Google AdMob banner ads**. AdMob uses your device's advertising ID to serve them, as described in our [privacy policy](/privacy.html). It never receives your peptide or dose data. **Pro removes ads** and that data collection.

## What you get

- **Reconstitution and dose calculator** with a visual 30/50/100-unit syringe diagram
- **Reminders** with Mark Taken and Skip on the notification, which work offline and survive reboots
- **Vial tracking** with low-supply alerts and expiry warnings
- **Injection site log, weight trend, and progress photos**
- **Free for up to 2 peptides.** Pro is $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr.
