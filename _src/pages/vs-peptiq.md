# PepMinder vs PeptIQ

## Where PeptIQ wins

- **AI features**: a chat companion, protocol audit, and a vial label scanner.
- **Wearables**: Oura, WHOOP (beta), Apple Health, and Health Connect.
- **Education**: a large peptide library and community forum.
- **Web app and sync.**

## Where PepMinder wins

- **You can actually try it free.** "No free option" is the most common complaint in PeptIQ's low-star reviews.
- **About a quarter of the monthly price** for Pro, or **$49.99 once** with nothing to renew.
- **No account and no questionnaire.** Install it and start logging.
- **Privacy.** Nothing about your protocol leaves your phone.

## Which should you pick?

- Choose **PeptIQ** if AI and wearable integrations are the point for you and the price fits.
- Choose **PepMinder** if you want an accurate calculator, reliable reminders, and a private log without paying first.
