# PepMinder vs Dose Track

<div class="disclosure"><strong>Disclosure:</strong> we make PepMinder. Dose Track is a well-built app with features we don't have yet, and we say so. Details checked on Google Play, the App Store, and dosetrack.app on <strong>October 8, 2026</strong>.</div>

**Short version:** both are private, no-account trackers. **Dose Track** is built around **estimated level charts** from a large compound library. **PepMinder** is built around **fast, accurate logging**, with a syringe diagram, a lower price, and less to learn.

## Where Dose Track wins

- **Level charts** are its core feature, backed by a library of 600+ compounds, with a "Dial-In" simulator.
- **Health data**: HealthKit and Health Connect, plus a daily journal.
- **A slightly bigger free tier** (3 medications).

## Where PepMinder wins

- **Less to learn.** Some Dose Track reviewers say there's "a ton going on" when they just want to log doses.
- **Cheaper Pro**: $39.99/yr versus $69.99/yr.
- **Visual syringe diagram** and a water suggestion that lands your dose on a clean mark.
- **Progress photos** with side-by-side comparison, and an **Android widget**.

## Which should you pick?

- Choose **Dose Track** if estimated level and steady-state charts are what you care about most.
- Choose **PepMinder** if you want the fastest path from reminder to logged dose, with a lower price.
