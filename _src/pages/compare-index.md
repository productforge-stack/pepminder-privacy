## What makes PepMinder different

- **No account, ever.** Install it and start. There's no email, no sign-up, and no server holding your dosing history.
- **Works fully offline.** The calculator, reminders, and logs all work with no connection.
- **Free for up to 2 peptides**, and Pro is $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr, among the lowest prices in the category. Prefer no subscription? **Lifetime Pro is $49.99 once.**
- **Titration built in, free**: enter dose steps from your provider and every reminder follows them.
- **Syringe math you can see**: a 30/50/100-unit diagram with the fill line marked.
- **No dosing advice, no product links.** PepMinder is a neutral notebook and calculator.
