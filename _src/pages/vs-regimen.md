# PepMinder vs Regimen

<div class="disclosure"><strong>Disclosure:</strong> we make PepMinder. Regimen is an excellent app for analysis, and we say so below. Details checked on Google Play and the App Store on <strong>October 8, 2026</strong>.</div>

## Where Regimen wins

- **Titration and complex schedules**, with reminders before each dose change.
- **Analysis**: estimated level curves, "Signals" trend detection, and bloodwork overlays.
- **Health Connect and Apple Health** integration.
- **Cross-device sync** and a web app.

## Where PepMinder wins

- **Offline and private.** No account, and your data is never on a server. Regimen reviewers specifically complain that nothing loads without internet.
- **More free, for less.** 2 peptides free (versus 1), and Pro is cheaper at $39.99/yr versus $49.99/yr.
- **Simpler.** Fewer screens between you and a logged dose.

## Which should you pick?

- Choose **Regimen** if you're on a titration schedule (common with GLP-1s) or want level curves and lab analysis, and you're comfortable with an account and a connection.
- Choose **PepMinder** if you want reliable reminders, accurate syringe math, and a clean log that's private and works anywhere.
